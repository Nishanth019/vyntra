Delete your Manaze account

From the app

  1. Open Manaze and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

By email

If you can't use the app, email bhukyanishanth69@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my Manaze account". We'll process it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login is deleted immediately: you can no longer sign in, and your access to every gym is removed.
  • Your phones stop receiving notifications.
  • Your personal details (name, email, mobile) are permanently deleted within 30 days.

Gym records you entered (members, plans, payments, attendance) belong to the gym and remain available to its other admins. If you are the gym's only admin and want the gym's data deleted too, mention it in your email.
