Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Try before you pay

Every new login gets a 15-day free trial with everything unlocked, so you can check that Vyntra suits your gym before paying anything.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel any time. On the website, open Subscription and tap Turn Off Auto-Renew. You can also cancel the UPI AutoPay mandate from your UPI app.
  • Plans bought in the Android app are cancelled from the Google Play Store (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).
  • After cancelling, no further payments are taken. You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for; after that your data stays viewable but read-only.

Refunds

  • Payments for a period that has started are not refundable, and we do not give partial refunds for unused days.
  • If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or charged after cancelling, email us within 7 days of the charge and we will refund it in full.
  • Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
  • Refunds for Google Play purchases are handled under Google Play's refund policy.
