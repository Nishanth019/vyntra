Questions

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Sign up and everything is unlocked for 15 days. Pick a plan only if Vyntra works for you.

Can I use it on my phone and computer?

Yes. Use the Android app at the gym and the website on any computer, with the same login and data.

I have my members in Excel. Do I have to type them again?

No. Download our template (or use your own sheet), upload it, and Vyntra checks every row before importing.

Can my front-desk staff use it?

Yes. Add them as admins with their own login. As the owner, you can block or remove them any time.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your data stays safe and viewable. It becomes read-only until you renew, so nothing is ever lost.

How do I cancel?

Turn off auto-renew from the Subscription page any time. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
