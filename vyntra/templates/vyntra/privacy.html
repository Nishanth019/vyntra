Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Vyntra is a gym management app. Gym owners and their staff ("admins") use it to manage members, plans, payments and attendance. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data we collect

  • Admin accounts: name, email address, mobile number and password (stored encrypted).
  • Gym and member records entered by admins: member name, member ID, mobile number, email, date of birth, gender, emergency contact and address, membership plans, payments and attendance.
  • Device data: a push notification token, so we can send notifications to the admin's phone.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and the app has no ads.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, member management, payment and attendance records, reports and insights.
  • To send admins notifications such as plan expiries, dues and daily summaries.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Who processes it

Data is stored on our servers hosted by DigitalOcean and Aiven (Singapore). Push notifications are delivered through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service. Subscription payments are processed by Razorpay on the website and by Google Play in the Android app; we never see or store your card or UPI details. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Each gym's data is only visible to that gym's admins.

Gym members' data

Member records are entered and controlled by the gym. Members who want their data corrected or removed should contact their gym, or write to us at the email below.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the gym uses Vyntra. Admins can delete their account from the app (Profile → Delete Account) or as described on our account deletion page.

Children

The app is meant for gym businesses and is not directed at children under 13.
